2 · Rewire: the reason you’re stuck

…or why your life doesn’t look the way you want it to

  1. 1

    Your thoughts create feelings. Think “I’m not good enough”, and you feel anxious and small.

  2. 2

    Feelings influence actions. Anxious and small, you hide, people-please, or put it off.

  3. 3

    Actions create your reality. Nothing changes. The chances pass you by.

  1. 4

    Then your reality influences your thoughts. “See? I knew I wasn’t good enough.” And you get stuck on a repeated loop that feels impossible to break.

Questions

When do doors open?

The intimate group program opens in January for the Feb/March cohort, with only 15 spots. The online community opens soon, and 1:1 is currently full, with spots potentially opening in January. Waitlist members hear first, before it’s announced anywhere else.

How long does it take?

8 weeks. The lessons are all short videos, 10 minutes on average. You do what you have capacity for at the time. If it takes longer, it takes longer.

Is it live, or can I do it in my own time?

Both. The lessons are self-paced in the Breakthrough app, with a new week opening as you go. There are weekly group Zoom calls to keep you accountable for the 8 weeks, and a community message board to support you between calls.

How long do I keep the resources?

You have access for life. The community coaching calls that go with it run for 8 weeks.

Does joining the waitlist mean I’m signing up?

Nope. It just means you’ll be the first to know when doors open. You decide then.
