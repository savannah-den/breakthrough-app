Every phone notification

What lands on your clients’ phones (and yours), in the order they meet them, and what it looks like on a lock screen. Tapping a notification opens the right place in the app, or Zoom for call reminders.

The rules

  • One kind reminder a day at most, at the time they pick in their Profile, in their own timezone.
  • Call reminders are extra: the day before, 2 hours before and 15 minutes before every call.
  • On by default. They tap Allow at the end of the welcome steps, and can turn them off any time in Profile.
  • Nothing at all if they’ve turned notifications off. iPhones need the app on the home screen first.
