How often: my recommendation

  • Essentials always send: login code, welcome, agreement, call reminders, session recaps.
  • Everything else: at most 1 a day and 3 a week, never on a day they have a call.
  • Only between 9am and 7pm their time (call reminders excepted).
  • Gone quiet? Two check-ins max (day 7 and 14), then email stops and the app’s gentle notifications carry on.
  • Their choice: a switch in Profile to turn off check-ins and nudges, plus an unsubscribe link in those emails. Essentials stay on.
  • Every subject line starts with their name and ends with “Savannah - Colour Me Neurospicy”, so they can always find you in their inbox.
  • Call reminders: email + phone notification the day before and 2 hours before; 15 minutes before is a phone notification only. Times show their time in bold, with yours underneath.
  • Phone vs email: daily nudges stay as phone notifications only. Email is for things worth keeping.
