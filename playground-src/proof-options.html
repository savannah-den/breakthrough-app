Pick one (or mix two)

Proof Portfolio icon options

The current one is a little jar with a sparkle, which doesn't say "proof" on its own. Here are nine other ideas. Each shows the big cover from the Resources page and the small icon used on Home, the Becoming Book and the course list. Tell Claude the number you like.

Option 1

The evidence folder

A real portfolio folder, notes and wins spilling out, with a big tick on the front.

What it says: "I'm collecting the evidence, and it's adding up."

Option 2

The snapshot stack

A little pile of polaroids, the top one a sunny "I did it!" moment.

What it says: "Snapshots of me being the new me."

Option 3

The star jar

A jar filling with stars. Every win drops one in, and you watch it fill.

What it says: "Every win counts. Look how full it's getting."

Option 4

The PROVEN stamp

A rubber stamp thumping down "PROVEN" on a note. Fun, punchy, very clear.

What it says: "That old story? Proven wrong. Stamped."

Option 5

Tipping the scales

One small old belief on one side, a growing pile of proof on the other, and the scale tips.

What it says: "The proof is starting to outweigh the old story."

Option 6

Keep the receipts

A receipt printing out your wins line by line. Total at the bottom: I CAN.

What it says: "When my brain argues, I've got the receipts."

Option 7

Brick by brick

Each win is a brick. They stack into a solid new foundation.

What it says: "I'm building a new belief, one win at a time."

Option 8

Spot the proof

A magnifying glass finding sparkles everywhere. This is your RAS filter at work.

What it says: "Now I'm looking for it, I see proof everywhere."

Option 9

Case closed

A clipboard. The old belief gets crossed out and the new evidence writes in underneath.

What it says: "Old story crossed out. New evidence in."

Tell Claude: "Option 6" (or "6 but with the stamp from 4")
