        How to use it

        1. Fill things in as if you were a client, then open the coach dashboard to see how your answers look from your side.
        2. Ask Claude to change anything: wording, colours, steps, new tools. Changes land here first.
        3. When you love it, tell Claude "send it live". It goes to the preview link, then to your real app.

        Playground differences: you're logged in automatically, the AI Coach Sav buttons are switched off, and videos open in a new tab instead of playing inside the app.
